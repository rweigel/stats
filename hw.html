1.1 Objective Interpretation of Probability

The objective interpretation of probability is that the the probability of event A is the limit of the relative frequency of A, Rf​=n(A)/n, as the number of experiments, n, used to compute the relative frequency approaches ∞:

P(A)=n→∞lim​nn(A)​

The following program computes the relative frequency of heads for a trial of n tosses where a zero or 1 is randomly selected with equal probability.
